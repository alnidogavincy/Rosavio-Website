Rosa damascena is cut by hand between five and nine in the morning, before the sun burns off the lightest molecules. It takes roughly 3,500 kg of petals to distil a single kilogram of rose otto for Rosavia. Everything the finished Extrait de Parfume is capable of becoming is decided here, in the field, before anyone has touched a bottle.
